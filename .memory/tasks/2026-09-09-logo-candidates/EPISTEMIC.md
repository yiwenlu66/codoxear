# Epistemic model

## Phenomenon
The user wants the original Codoxear icon identity — a folded paper document with a small terminal prompt — executed as calm, well-proportioned flat stationery in the cream/warm-gray/terracotta palette. Both rounds that left that identity failed: round one's variations were correct in direction but heavy in execution (blocky 24px contour, oversized low prompt, noisy duplicate tile border); the fresh abstract round (ears, bridges, tethers) was rejected outright as "all very ugly."

## Mechanism
At app-icon sizes this product's identity is the old screenshot mark: an elegant white page with softly rounded corners and a very small `>_` near mid-left. What made the old icon good was proportion and restraint, not its glossy purple tile. What made round-one 01 Paper Outline merely acceptable was its blockiness: heavy uniform contour, a prompt at ~45% page width sitting low, and a second stroked tile inside the tile. The refinement axis is therefore drawing quality — contour weight ~12–13px (of 512), page height ~60% of tile at a calmer ~0.73 width/height ratio, prompt ~30% of page width near midheight, one tile surface only — not new symbolism.
