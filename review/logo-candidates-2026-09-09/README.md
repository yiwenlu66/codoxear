# Codoxear logo candidates — 2026-09-09

This directory is a non-production review set. None of these files are referenced by the application, manifest, or favicon routes.

| Candidate | SVG | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| 01 — Paper Outline | `01-paper-outline.svg` | The most literal evolution of the existing flat mark: a smaller, calm document outline with a compact clay terminal prompt. It fits the paper theme while borrowing clay only for the active cue. |
| 02 — Clay Solid | `02-clay-solid.svg` | Makes the current default theme recognizable at a glance. The large, plain paper document preserves the old icon’s hierarchy; the terminal cue remains dark and subordinate. |
| 03 — Slate Monogram | `03-slate-monogram.svg` | The strongest high-contrast option. A monochrome slate tile gives the document silhouette the visual authority the current oversized outline lacks. |
| 04 — Paper Reverse | `04-paper-reverse.svg` | A dark-mode-ready e-ink option. The warm-charcoal field and cream page align directly with paper dark, with clay used only on the terminal cue. |
| 05 — Clay Tab | `05-clay-tab.svg` | The boldest document-first mark. The folded clay tab identifies the dog-ear at small sizes without adding shadows or a second outline. |

All candidates use a 512-unit square, a 108-unit rounded tile safe area, a document occupying roughly 53% of tile width, and terminal strokes no thinner than 24 units (3px when rasterized to 64px). They intentionally use only flat fills and a small theme-palette color set.

Open `comparison.svg` for the labeled large/small contact sheet, or inspect `comparison.png` for its rendered preview.
