# Codoxear fresh logo candidates — 2026-09-09

This is a non-production exploration set. Nothing here is referenced by the application, favicon, manifest, or theme assets.

Open **`comparison.svg`** (or `comparison.png`) for the color 180px/64px/32px and monochrome 32px review. Each candidate has deterministic vector source, a one-color SVG, and 180px, 64px, 32px, and 16px PNG renders. `build.py` regenerates every derived artifact with `rsvg-convert`.

## Construction constraints met

- 512 × 512 vectors with full-bleed background tiles; every foreground mark is held inside the 80%-diameter maskable safe circle.
- Flat solid fills only: no gradients, shadows, filters, gloss, text, or ornamental detail inside a mark.
- Each direction has a different outer silhouette and has a direct monochrome reduction (`*-mono.svg`, `*-mono-32.png`).
- No candidate is a page/file icon. Folded Passage deliberately retains the *dog-ear operation* as an architectural joint, not as a document outline. Ribbon is the sole bookmark-derived silhouette and its exit tail remains visible at 32px.
- The seven use clay, paper, slate, and warm-dark values as semantic hierarchy rather than recoloring one construction.

## Recommendation

**Advance 01 — Relay Ear** to a real home-screen comparison alongside **02 — Folded Passage** as the practical alternate. Relay Ear is the cleanest relationship among product name, listening, relay, and a live connector; it remains legible as an asymmetric spiral plus endpoint at 32px. Folded Passage is the more conservative continuation of the dog-ear history and has the strongest monochrome mass at small sizes.

The decision should include a home-screen test against neighboring developer tools. Relay Ear's residual collision is audio/accessibility; Folded Passage's is doorway/home. No trademark or competitive-logo clearance is included in this concept review.
